The largest consumer market in the world.

More than 330 million consumers, the world’s biggest economy and buyers in every industry.

Why the USA.

Roughly 340 million people and an economy of around $30 trillion.

Strong spending across every region, and buyers who actively seek new products, suppliers and partners with a clear value story.

Diverse regional markets

From the West Coast to Texas to the Northeast, each region has its own buyers, ports and demand.

Deep buyer and distribution networks

Retail chains, wholesalers, distributors and e-commerce platforms give many routes to market.

Capital and investment

Deep capital markets and a large pool of investors and acquirers for growing companies.

Next door to Canada

Canadian-based companies can serve US buyers by road, rail and sea, and use CUSMA where goods qualify.

Open to new suppliers

Buyers seek new products and partners, so a clear offer and the right duty plan win business.

Ports and gateways

Pacific, Gulf and Atlantic ports reach every region from Asia, the Middle East and Africa.

Trade deals and programs.

  • Reciprocal trade agreements have been published with partners including Indonesia, Argentina, Bangladesh and Jordan.
  • Japan, Korea, Taiwan and the EU have 15% arrangements on qualifying goods.
  • AGOA gives eligible African countries duty-free access and has been extended, with a review under way.
  • Many countries face 10% or 12.5% Section 301 tariffs since July 2026.
