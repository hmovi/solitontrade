Services.From first question to first shipment.

Use one service or the full path. Every engagement starts with understanding your product and goals.

Trade and market analysis

Know the numbers before you ship, sign or invest.

  • Market size, demand and competitor review
  • Tariff, duty and landed-cost modelling
  • Trade-agreement eligibility (CUSMA, CETA, CPTPP and new deals)
  • Go or no-go recommendation

Import readiness and compliance

Get your product across the border the right way.

  • Tariff classification, valuation and origin checks
  • Importer-of-record and non-resident importer set-up
  • Labelling, safety and product standards review
  • Coordination with licensed customs brokers

Supplier sourcing

Find suppliers in Canada and the USA you can rely on.

  • Supplier search and shortlisting
  • Vetting, samples and reference checks
  • Pricing and contract negotiation
  • Ongoing supplier management

Buyers, partners and acquisitions

Reach the companies that will buy, resell or join you.

  • Distributor, agent and joint-venture partner search
  • Buyer introductions and qualification
  • Identifying companies to acquire or invest in
  • Partnership terms and due-diligence support

Sales and marketing support

Turn market access into revenue.

  • Positioning, pricing and channel strategy
  • Lead generation and trade-show planning
  • Bilingual and local-market content for Canada
  • Sales support for your first accounts

Logistics and distribution

Move goods efficiently across the border.

  • Freight, warehousing and 3PL selection
  • Cross-border and last-mile routes
  • Retail, wholesale and e-commerce distribution
  • Supply chain risk and tariff planning

Financing assistance

Fund the shipment, the stock and the growth.

  • Trade finance and working capital options
  • Letters of credit and payment terms
  • Export credit and government program guidance
  • Introductions to lenders and investors

Entry structure and investment

Set up the right way to operate and invest.

  • Branch, subsidiary or partner structure options
  • Tax, banking and incorporation introductions
  • Investment screening and regulatory notes
  • Referral to legal and accounting specialists

Trade network and introductions

Meet the people who make deals move.

  • Customs brokers, forwarders and carriers
  • Trade lawyers, accountants and banks
  • Chambers of commerce and industry associations
  • Government trade offices and programs
