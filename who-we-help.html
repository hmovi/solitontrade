Exporters and manufacturers

Africa

Exporters and manufacturers in Africa looking for buyers, distributors and duty advantages in Canada and the USA.

  • AfCFTA: 54 African countries support the continental market, which lets suppliers build scale before exporting.
  • AGOA: the US preference program was renewed in 2026, and recent reports put the extension through 2028. It remains under review.
  • Canada offers duty-free access for least developed countries.
Gulf, Levant and North Africa

MENA

Companies in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Türkiye, Jordan, Egypt and across MENA that want North American buyers, partners or investment destinations.

  • Canada–UAE CEPA: negotiated in 47 days and signed in October 2026. Expected in force in 2027.
  • Canada is also negotiating with Saudi Arabia and Türkiye.
  • US: Jordan’s Agreement on Reciprocal Trade was published in July 2026.
India, ASEAN, Japan, Korea, Oceania

Asia Pacific

Manufacturers and trading companies in India, Southeast Asia, Japan, Korea, Australia and New Zealand.

  • Canada–Indonesia CEPA: signed in September 2025.
  • Canada is negotiating with ASEAN and the Philippines, and talks with India are gaining momentum.
  • Canada is a CPTPP member, and a Team Canada mission to Japan took place in 2026.
  • US: reciprocal trade arrangements with Indonesia, Malaysia and others, and 15% arrangements with Japan, Korea and Taiwan.

Why use a trade advisor.

The cost of a wrong first move is higher than the cost of good advice.

Rules that change monthly

US tariff actions, the CUSMA review and Canada’s new import rules move fast. An advisor tracks them so you plan on current facts.

Fewer costly mistakes

Wrong classification, origin or valuation can mean back duties and penalties. We check before you ship.

Pre-vetted network

We already know the brokers, forwarders, lenders, distributors and buyers worth your time.

Independent advice

We do not own the freight, the warehouse or the supplier, so our advice does not depend on selling you one.

One team, two markets

Canada and the USA share a border but not their rules. You get one plan that covers both.

Test first, pay less duty

Validate demand and landed cost before you commit, and match your product to the trade agreement with the best terms.
