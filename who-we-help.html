Who we help.Companies entering North America from Africa, MENA and Asia Pacific.

We also help Canadian and US companies source suppliers, find partners and invest abroad.

Africa

Exporters and manufacturers in Africa looking for buyers, distributors and duty advantages in Canada and the USA.

Trade deals and programs

  • AfCFTA: 54 African countries support the continental market, which lets suppliers build scale before exporting.
  • AGOA: the US preference program for eligible sub-Saharan countries was renewed in 2026, and recent reports put the extension through the end of 2028. It remains under review.
  • Canada offers duty-free access for least developed countries.

Eligibility, rules of origin and US tariff layers decide whether a product qualifies. We check this first.

Middle East and North Africa

Companies in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Türkiye, Jordan, Egypt, Morocco and across MENA that want North American buyers, partners or investment destinations.

Trade deals and programs

  • Canada–UAE CEPA: negotiated in a record 47 days and signed in October 2026. It is expected to enter into force in 2027.
  • Canada is also negotiating with Saudi Arabia and Türkiye.
  • US: Jordan’s Agreement on Reciprocal Trade was published in July 2026.

Gulf investors and exporters can use our Vancouver base as a Canadian entry point and our network to reach the USA.

Asia Pacific

Manufacturers and trading companies in India, Southeast Asia, Japan, Korea, Australia and New Zealand.

Trade deals and programs

  • Canada–Indonesia CEPA: signed in September 2025.
  • Canada is negotiating with ASEAN and the Philippines, and talks with India are gaining momentum.
  • Canada is a CPTPP member, and Team Canada missions to Japan took place in 2026.
  • US: reciprocal trade arrangements with Indonesia, Malaysia and others, and 15% arrangements with Japan, Korea and Taiwan.

Vancouver is Canada’s Pacific gateway. We help Asia Pacific firms choose between Canadian and US routes.
